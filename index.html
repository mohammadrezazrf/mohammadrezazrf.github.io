Major Projects

Endoscopic Capsule Robots

This project focuses on the design, prototyping, and control of magnetically actuated capsule endoscopes. A major part of this research involved the design and experimental validation of a biopsy mechanism for capsule robots. Since both capsule localization and biopsy actuation rely on magnetic fields, the design aimed to minimize interference between these functions while maintaining a fail-safe biopsy mechanism. In addition, I investigated optimal control strategies for capsule robots to minimize the energy consumption of the actuation coils. This project was part of my M.Sc. thesis at Sharif University of Technology and resulted in two publications.
